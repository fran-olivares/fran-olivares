---

**Olivares is to AI what Linux and systemd are to an operating system.** Claude Code, Codex, Grok, OpenCode, Ollama and the models are the userland. Olivares runs them as sessions, connects them to your folders, data, secrets and people, and records what they did.

- One self-hosted Go binary with the console built in. SQLite or PostgreSQL. Linux and macOS.
- Each session runs confined to its folder, with approvals and a hash-chained audit trail.
- Open core: AGPL-3.0 core, Apache-2.0 connectors. Business and Enterprise fund the work.
