## Olivares AI

**Ground truth for enterprise AI.** Know what your AI touches, decide what it can do, and prove it.

[![License](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-AGPL--3.0-F08000?style=flat&labelColor=1A1A19)](https://github.com/olivaresai/olivares)

Integrate, manage and secure AI in your enterprise — **built around Claude and Claude Code**. A single self-hosted Go binary with the console embedded: context, granular permissions, policy enforcement and a signed audit ledger for the AI running on your infrastructure — on-prem or fully air-gapped, nothing leaves your perimeter. It complements Claude; it does not compete.
